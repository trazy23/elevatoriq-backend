Pilot Overview

ElevatorIQ is providing a complimentary 90-day pilot of its independent elevator procurement intelligence platform to Trinity Health Oakland Hospital. This agreement defines the scope, deliverables, and terms of that pilot. There is no cost, no commitment to purchase, and no vendor contact involved.

Recommended Starting Scope — Module A: Invoice Review

Trinity Health Oakland will select one ElevatorIQ module for the initial pilot. The recommended starting point is Module A: Invoice Review.

  • Rocky uploads 3–6 months of Otis maintenance invoices as PDF files
  • ElevatorIQ delivers a full structured analysis report within 48 hours
  • The report includes: line-item audit, labor rate review, callback billing analysis, escalation clause identification, and a risk-flagged executive summary
What Trinity Health Oakland Provides
  • PDF copies of Otis maintenance invoices (3–6 months preferred)
  • Elevator count and basic equipment list, if available (not required — enhances analysis but is optional)
  • A 30-minute debrief call after report delivery to review findings with Trey
What ElevatorIQ Provides
  • Full independent analysis report per upload, delivered by email within 48 hours
  • Vendor-neutral findings — ElevatorIQ has no affiliation with Otis or any elevator manufacturer or service provider
  • Confidential report — not shared with any third party, ever
Confidentiality

All documents uploaded to ElevatorIQ and all report findings are strictly confidential. ElevatorIQ has no vendor affiliations and does not share, sell, or disclose client data to any third party under any circumstances.

Case Study Clause

If the pilot surfaces meaningful findings, Trinity Health Oakland agrees to provide a written testimonial and permits ElevatorIQ to reference anonymized findings in marketing materials and case studies. The hospital name, Rocky Alazazi's name, and any identifying information will not be used without explicit written approval from Trinity Health Oakland.

Cost & Duration

Cost: Complimentary. No invoice will be issued. No payment is due or expected.

Duration: 90 days from the date of first document upload.

No automatic continuation: This pilot does not auto-renew into a paid arrangement. Any ongoing access will be discussed and agreed separately after the pilot period.
