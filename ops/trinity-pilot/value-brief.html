Most facility directors have no independent way to verify what they're actually getting for that spend. The invoice arrives. It gets approved. The pattern repeats — for years. ElevatorIQ changes that.

Specific issues it surfaces in hospital accounts:
Callback labor billed outside contract scope — full-maintenance contracts are supposed to cover callbacks, but vendors routinely reclassify them as "out of scope"
Proprietary parts markups — components billed at 40–80% above OEM cost on units where market alternatives exist
Automatic escalation clauses compounding unchecked — 3–5% annual increases over 5+ years can add $250–$400 per unit per month without any renegotiation
Units over-contracted for their actual duty cycle — mixed-vintage fleets commonly include elevators on full-maintenance contracts they don't need, at 30–50% excess cost per unit
